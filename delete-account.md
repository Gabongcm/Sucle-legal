# Delete your SUCLE account

SUCLE respects your right to delete your account and your data. This page explains how.

## Option 1: Delete inside the app

1. Open the SUCLE app and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**, then tap the **Settings** (gear) icon.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to **mentorgabong@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my SUCLE account"**. Please send it from the email address linked to your SUCLE account and include your SUCLE username so we can verify you. We will confirm by email once the request is complete.

## What is deleted

- Your profile (name, username, biography, country and profile picture link).
- Your posts, Reels, comments, likes, follows and saved items.
- Your sign-in account (email and unique account ID).

## What may be kept

Some records may be kept for a limited time when we are required to by law or for security and fraud prevention, such as financial transaction records and moderation records. These are kept only as long as necessary and are not used for any other purpose.

## How long it takes

We delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days of your request.
