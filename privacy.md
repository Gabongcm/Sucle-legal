# SUCLE Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 22, 2026

**Developer:** SUCLE, Tanzania

**Contact:** mentorgabong@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how SUCLE (the "App") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our mobile app and related services. By using the App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 1. Who we are

SUCLE is a social media and video platform operated by SUCLE, based in Tanzania ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact us at mentorgabong@gmail.com.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign up with email and password or with Google Sign-In, we receive your email address, a unique account ID and, if you use Google, your Google display name and a link to your profile picture.

**Profile information you provide.** Display name, username, biography, country and other profile details you choose to add.

**Content you create.** Posts, comments, replies, likes, follows, saved items, reviews, Reels details (captions, hashtags, category, audio name) and any reports you submit.

**Usage information.** How you use the App, such as the Reels you watch, how long you watch, whether you finish a video, and your interactions. We use this to operate the App, improve recommendations and detect abuse.

**Device and technical information.** Device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate location derived from your connection, and diagnostic data, collected through Google and Firebase services.

**Advertising information.** If we enable advertising in the App, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.

**Payments and earnings.** If we offer virtual coins, gifts or withdrawals, purchases of virtual coins are processed by Google Play. We do not store your card number or CVV. For withdrawals we may collect payout details and identity verification information required by law and by our payment providers.

**Verification information.** If you apply for a verified badge, we may collect identity documents and a selfie. These are stored securely, are visible only to authorized staff, and are never shown publicly.

## 3. How we use information

- To create and manage your account and provide the App's features.
- To show you content, including recommendations based on your activity.
- To keep the platform safe: moderation, reports, fraud and abuse prevention, and enforcing our rules.
- To process purchases, gifts, earnings and withdrawals, if and when these features are available.
- To show and measure advertising, if and when advertising is enabled.
- To communicate with you about your account, security and policy changes.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## 4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

- **Public content.** Your profile, posts, Reels, comments and other content you publish are visible to other users according to your privacy settings.
- **Service providers.** We use Google Firebase and Google Cloud (authentication, database, hosting and related services). If and when these features are available, we also use Google Play (purchases), Google AdMob (advertising) and payout providers. They process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.
- **Moderation and safety.** Authorized staff may review reported content and related account information.
- **Legal reasons.** We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users, or to enforce our policies.
- **Business changes.** If the service is transferred or merged, your information may be transferred, and we will notify you.

## 5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure operated by Google, in data centers that may be located outside Tanzania. Your information may be processed in other countries where our providers operate, with appropriate safeguards.

## 6. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we must keep certain records for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons (for example financial transaction records and moderation records), which are kept only as long as necessary.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction.** You can view and edit your profile information in the App.
- **Account and data deletion.** You can delete your account inside the App (Profile, then Settings, then Delete account), or by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page, or by emailing mentorgabong@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my SUCLE account".
- **Privacy controls.** You can manage who can see your content and block or report other users in the App.
- **Advertising choices.** If advertising is enabled, you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings.
- **Other rights.** Depending on the laws that apply to you, including the applicable laws of Tanzania, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal information, or to request a copy of it. Contact us to exercise these rights.

## 8. Children

SUCLE is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections, access controls, role-based staff permissions and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. User-generated content and moderation

You can report content or users inside the App. We review reports and may remove content or restrict accounts that violate our rules. Moderation decisions may involve automated tools and human review.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version in the App and on this page with a new effective date, and notify you of significant changes.
